## Six review conditions

- **S1:** `baselinePair` uses the existing source-A / exactly-one-restore-B lifecycle. Both probes follow restart. Quiescence includes the product's actual `memoryDue()` value and all pending extraction/model work. A cannot create a Task, uncertain/applied mutation or changed sink; its failure remains evidence and B stays unexercised. Restore never resets campaign accounting. Initialize the source's payload guard before setup; future-input lists must be frozen by the eventual live driver.
- **S2:** maintenance matches B's `main.message` source ID and its handled sequence, since the product does not put `input.handled` marker IDs into `source_event_ids`. A committed non-CONTEXT_COMPACTION cycle is required. Deadline outcomes distinguish absent trigger, running, retry-pending failure and terminal failure, preserving every failed attempt. The forecast uses normal 8-turn/60-second/120-second timing, with the source's critical-feedback exception, and reports opportunities rather than achieved exit or coverage.
- **S3:** route metadata separates product UNSUPPORTED from #120 HARNESS_EXCLUDED. Real fixture coverage exercises an owned note, RESOURCE_PRESENT waiting, scripted file creation, Task `read`, and `submit_result`, without a model write. That proves the route, not source-authority semantics of a live model. Note-file hashes/read receipts and Task interpretations remain separate oracle sources. Question/answer and World exact-approval case choreography must be frozen before their live pilot; existing product routes alone do not prove semantic coverage.
- **S4:** [source inspection](../../../evidence/issue122-reuse-audit.json) retains the three-model lifecycle-v2 same-operation chain and one-write observation. It lacks a separately persisted contemporaneous old-process exit witness: C12b is required. New process witnesses query the owned OS identity and retain immutable STOPPED/RUNNING receipts. A hash-only evidence inventory reports identity review still required; it never claims all interruption semantics passed. Restart exports distinguish direct same-domain and checkpoint restore.
- **S5:** three necessary archives were streamed read-only through the existing verifier, including all file hashes/sizes; no SUT, historical controller or PG was started. Access is logged privately. Only small synthetic evidence selections entered quarantine; private-A packet integrity inspection yielded sanitized metadata only. Other historical rows remain report-only, with no regrading or altered labels.
- **S6:** optional `issue122-budget-v1` policy extends the existing durable campaign, bounding model/lane/aggregate USD and reservations, preserving unknown-usage charges across descendants, rejecting lineage rebinding and reserving an entire lane allowance at admission. Connectivity has at most three reservations per model. Frozen rates and billed-output bounds are required; reasoning uses a verified maximum if the requested cap is not a verified bound. The send guard applies frozen rates both to reservations and settlement. Its wall-clock dispatch windows are conservatively stricter than active time; offline/archive/review work precedes dispatch. No policy has been approved for real use.

## Semantic extension and evidence limits

`secretary-issue122-semantic-v1` separates natural access, current/history, maintenance-current, use-current, authority, text survival, item lineage, obligations, effects and source identity. A grade is bound to the exact case/packet and expected assertions; PASS/FAIL require hash-verified evidence references. Unsupported capability, harness exclusion and deferred time are manifest classifications, not semantic success. Missing stable item identities remain NOT_TESTED. Cold proof requires all four actual request surfaces absent, no opaque state, semantic-absence review and an original-source read/use; a correct guess is not enough. Physical effect violations remain authoritative even if judge prose disagrees. Maintenance results and use results never overwrite each other.

Development controls cover stale versus historical truth, invented versus faithful authority, and duplicate versus observed/unknown effects. Exported control IDs have no polarity; expected labels stay in the private key, outside SUT input. These public authored controls are dev-exposed, not held out. Failed calibration blocks semantic grading, not SUT evidence retention. Before any browser judging, bind hashes and budgets in the reviewed live freeze; maximum 12 SUT batches plus one calibration, up to three identical mechanical attempts and no semantic retry. The old #121 v3 and accepted grades are unchanged.

The current per-case forecast has **no physical request bytes**, no semantic absence proof and no completion cost guarantee. Master then chooses RC coverage or C16 deferral and approves a concrete freeze/budget. Full matrix repetition, product repairs and merge have separate gates.
